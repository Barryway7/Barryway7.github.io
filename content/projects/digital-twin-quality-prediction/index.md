Undergraduate thesis (Advisors: Prof. Minghai Jiao & Chang Liu, 11/2024–06/2025). Combined physics-based and data-driven modeling to predict the mechanical properties of steel products.

<!--more-->

- Modeled the effects of cold rolling and annealing on material properties using thermodynamic and kinetic empirical formulas.
- Integrated physics-derived features (grain size, dislocation density) into XGBoost, CatBoost, and TabTransformer models for property prediction.
- Optimized model hyperparameters and feature selection with evolutionary algorithms.
- Built an interactive digital twin in Unity to visualize process information and predict mechanical properties in real time.
