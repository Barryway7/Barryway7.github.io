Team leader, advised by Prof. Dongyue Chen (01/2022–09/2023). Built a real-time vision system to detect potentially harmful interactions among students.

<!--more-->

- Combined YOLOv5 and DeepSORT into a real-time multi-target tracking pipeline for identifying and tracking individuals in crowded scenes.
- Trained on a public violence dataset augmented with data our team collected and curated for middle- and high-school contexts.
- Deployed across a wide range of school camera systems, enabling real-time alerts so staff can intervene promptly in bullying incidents.
