Research assistantship with Assistant Prof. Zheming Gao (06/2023–08/2024). Developed a robust kernel-free classifier for imbalanced, high-noise datasets.

<!--more-->

- Proposed Pin-QSSVM, integrating the Quadratic Surface Support Vector Machine (QSSVM) framework with the pinball loss function for more flexible error penalization.
- Optimized the model via stochastic gradient descent and second-order cone programming.
- Evaluated on Iris, Heart Disease, German Credit, and proprietary healthcare/finance datasets: 11% higher accuracy and 9% lower misclassification error than traditional QSSVM, with a 14% F1 improvement on imbalanced data.
