I am a Master's student at the USC Viterbi School of Engineering, working with Prof. Paul Bogdan on non-Markovian dynamics of large language models and multi-agent LLM systems. Before USC, I received my BEng in Automation from Northeastern University, China.

I study LLMs and LLM agents as dynamical systems, with interests in fractional-order dynamics, failure prediction in multi-agent systems, neural operators, and learning-based control. I am currently applying to PhD programs.
